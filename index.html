Who we are

Big dreams deserve real opportunities.

Women and Girls Dream It Be It Network is a women-led organization based in Uganda, committed to elevating women and girls through access to technology, skills and a supportive community.

We've discovered remarkable talent among young innovators. Our purpose is to help that potential grow, connect and make a meaningful difference.
