Privacy, by design.

Ewuluguma does not require registration or login, and TERYAMUDO does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal information through the game. The game is intended to work entirely offline.

This policy describes the intended data practices of the Ewuluguma application published by TERYAMUDO.

01. Information we collect

We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, account credentials, precise location, contacts, photographs, microphone recordings, advertising identifiers or other personal information through Ewuluguma.

We do not operate game analytics, user profiling, behavioral tracking or advertising services in the app. We do not transmit gameplay information to TERYAMUDO servers.

If you voluntarily email us with a privacy question, your email address and the information you choose to send will be received through your email provider and used only to respond to your inquiry. This correspondence takes place outside the game.

02. Offline gameplay

Ewuluguma is designed to work without an internet connection. You can play its memory-matching levels without creating an account, signing in or connecting to an online service.

03. Device permissions

The game does not request runtime access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, files or other sensitive device capabilities for gameplay. It is designed without permission prompts.

04. Advertising and third parties

Ewuluguma does not display third-party advertisements and does not include advertising or analytics integrations as part of its intended gameplay. We do not sell or share player information with advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

When you download or update the app through an app store, the store and your operating system may process information independently under their own privacy policies. This is separate from data collection by Ewuluguma.

05. Data retention and deletion

TERYAMUDO does not maintain player accounts or a server-side database of player information, so we have no player account records to retain or delete.

If the app stores gameplay progress or preferences on your device, that information stays on the device and is not sent to us. You can remove locally stored app data using your device's application settings or by uninstalling the game, subject to your device's backup and restore behavior.

Emails you voluntarily send us are handled solely for correspondence. You may request deletion of that correspondence by contacting us at the address below, subject to applicable legal obligations.

06. Security

Our primary privacy safeguard is data minimization: the game is designed not to collect or transmit personal information. Any locally stored gameplay information is handled within the device's application storage environment. No online service is required to play.

07. Age suitability

Ewuluguma is intended for players aged 13 and above. It does not offer chat, user-generated public profiles or social account features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from players of any age.

Age suitability is a description of our intended audience, not a substitute for the age ratings or target-audience declarations determined through Google Play.

08. Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's features or data practices change or if legal requirements evolve. We will update the effective date shown above when changes are published. Material changes to data practices will be reflected in the app and applicable store disclosures.
